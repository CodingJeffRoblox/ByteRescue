# Code of Conduct

## Our Standard

ByteRescue is a community project. We want contributions, bug reports, questions, and discussions to remain respectful, constructive, and focused on improving the software.

Expected behavior includes:

- Being respectful and professional.
- Giving constructive technical feedback.
- Welcoming people with different experience levels.
- Respecting privacy and sensitive data.
- Keeping discussions focused on the project.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, threats, discrimination, personal attacks, doxxing, or intentionally disruptive conduct.

## Reporting

If you experience unacceptable behavior, contact the repository owner privately through GitHub. Please include enough information to understand what happened and avoid sharing unnecessary personal information.

Project maintainers may remove comments, close discussions, or restrict participation when necessary to keep the project productive and safe.
